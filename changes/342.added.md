The sprint page's "What changed" card now names the previous sprint in its heading ("What changed since Sprint N") and marks each moved measure as better or worse in words. Each line carries a plain sentence naming the measure and both numbers, the two values with their units, and a "better" or "worse" word for the six headline measures (stories delivered, points delivered, first-try rate, start to delivered, filed to delivered, days blocked). Stories superseded shows both numbers without a direction word. WIP violations no longer appear in the card.
